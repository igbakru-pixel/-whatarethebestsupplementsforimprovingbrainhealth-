<h1><strong>What Are the Best Supplements for Improving Brain Health?</strong></h1>

<h2><strong>Vitamins for brain health: what vitamins are good for the brain?</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">People ask what vitamins are good for the brain, what vitamins are good for brain health and what vitamins help memory.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Brain vitamins sell well, and each vitamin for brain health has a different case.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The vitamins for brain health with the best case are the B vitamins, vitamin D and, in a different sense, vitamin E and C as antioxidants.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Here is how each stands up.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Vitamin B12 and folate (B9), plus B6.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> The NIH reports that short-term studies suggest B6, B12 and folic acid do not help cognitive functioning in adults over 50, but a meta-analysis of longer studies suggests B vitamins may be associated with slowing cognitive decline. A McMaster Optimal Aging summary found folic acid plus B12 improved memory at two years in two studies, rated as low-strength evidence.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Vitamin D.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Deficiency is linked with poorer thinking, but proof that supplements help people with normal levels is weak.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Vitamin E.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Moderate-quality evidence from a single study suggests it may slow functional decline in people who already have Alzheimer's, according to the NIH. It is not shown to help healthy people.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Vitamin C.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Low levels are associated with poorer mood and focus, but supplements help mainly if you are low.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you ask what vitamins and minerals are good for the brain, add magnesium, iron and zinc to the list, again mostly when you are deficient.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Good vitamins for the brain, and any vitamin for brain performance, are the ones your body is short on, and a blood test will tell you more than a label.</span></p>

<h2><strong>Final thoughts</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The best supplement for brain health depends on whether you are missing something. Correcting a real deficiency in B12, vitamin D, iron or magnesium has the clearest payoff.
